This is a live copy of the window. Drag the title bar, press X, then tap the small square that appears. It can be dragged too, and the Extra tab’s text box renames it.

What you get

Gradients everywhere

Lime-to-mint accents on a dark, layered surface. Edit the Theme table to recolor all of it.

Hidden minimize button

Clicking X reveals a small, draggable square with text you choose. A tap brings the window back.

Dragging that holds up

One routine for mouse and touch, measured from on-screen positions so scaling never skews it.

Fault tolerant

Every method is wrapped. A bad option or a failed call warns and moves on instead of stopping your script.

Works on phones

The window scales to fit, sliders pause page scrolling while dragged, and touch input is handled throughout.

No image assets

Even the hexagon close button is built from frames, so nothing to upload and nothing that can fail to load.

Start in ten lines

Every method is covered in the documentation.
